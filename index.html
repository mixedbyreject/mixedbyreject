Hi, my name is Ross

And I’m a mixing and mastering engineer based in Prague, originally from Kyiv, collaborating remotely with artists worldwide since 2018.

I specialize in metal and modern heavy music — engineering punchy drums, heavy yet articulate guitars, and clear, impactful vocals. Every mix is built around the raw energy of the performance, keeping the dynamics, character, and depth alive.

I also regularly work with rap, punk, alternative, and rock projects. My credits include tracks released under Sony Music and Believe Music. Additionally, I can record session electric and bass guitar parts to solidify your arrangement.
